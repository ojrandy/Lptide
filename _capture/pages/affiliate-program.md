How the program works
Reach the qualifying cumulative order value, then submit a free affiliate application.
After approval, share the personal link or code with new customers.
A completed referred order creates a commission record using the rate active at completion.
After the configured hold period, commission becomes available for controlled checkout credit. Withdrawal availability is shown separately.
Important controls
Refunded or disputed referred orders void the related commission immediately.
Using affiliate commission credit disables all coupons, campaigns, member pricing, and customer-service discounts for that order.
Customer support handles account questions through a dedicated case conversation. Financial review and payment records remain internal.
Withdrawal availability, supported payout assets, and networks are shown only when approved and enabled.
