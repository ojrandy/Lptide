Shipping rates by warehouse

Your shipping rule is based on the warehouse selected for the order. United States and Australia warehouse orders are charged by item count with different first-item rates. Asia warehouse orders are charged by weight and destination.

Shipping warehouse	Delivery destination	Initial charge	Additional charge
United States warehouse	All supported destinations	First item $30.00	+$5.00 per item
Australia warehouse	Australia	First item $20.00	+$5.00 per item
Asia warehouse	United States	First 0.5 kg $85.00	+$25.00 per 0.5 kg
Asia warehouse	Other supported destinations	First 0.5 kg $60.00	+$25.00 per 0.5 kg

Select the shipping warehouse and destination country at checkout to see the applicable estimate before placing the order.

Most orders ship within one business day after payment is confirmed. Orders placed before the daily fulfillment cutoff are usually prepared the same business day.

Carrier tracking

Shipments may use DHL, UPS, FedEx, or another regional carrier. Select the carrier when known; otherwise Auto detect checks the order record first and offers 17TRACK when no local match is available.

Order confirmation & tracking

After your package ships, the shipping confirmation includes a logistics tracking number. Tracking updates may take several hours to appear after the carrier first scans the package.

Lost or damaged shipments

If tracking has not updated for several business days, or the parcel arrives damaged, contact support with your order number and tracking number so the fulfillment team can review the shipment.
