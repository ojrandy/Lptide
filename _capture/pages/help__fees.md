Fees & payment overview

A clear overview of product pricing, handling fees, shipping estimates, and payment confirmation.

Product pricing

Product prices are shown on the catalog and product detail pages. The price and stock shown at checkout are the current values for the selected warehouse and destination.

Handling and shipping fees

Any handling or shipping fees are shown in the cart and checkout summary before you place the order. Shipping depends on the selected warehouse, destination country, item count, weight, and the active shipping rule.

Payment confirmation

An order is not treated as paid until the payment is confirmed in the order record. Keep your order number and payment reference when contacting support about a payment.

Need help?

For a fee or payment question, include the order number, destination country, selected warehouse, and a screenshot of the checkout summary when contacting support.
